# Ferryhopper MCP Integration - Example Prompts & Use Cases

This document provides example voice interactions and use cases for the Ferryhopper MCP integration in your voice assistant demo.

## 🎤 Example Voice Prompts

### Basic Ferry Search

**Simple Route Search:**
- *"What ferries go from Piraeus to Aegina tomorrow?"*
- *"Show me ferries from Santorini to Mykonos next Friday"*
- *"Find ferries from Barcelona to Ibiza on July 15th"*

**Time-Specific Searches:**
- *"What ferries depart from Piraeus to Aegina on Saturday morning?"*
- *"Are there any evening ferries from Mykonos to Santorini?"*
- *"Show me early morning ferries from Athens to Hydra"*

**Passenger-Specific:**
- *"Help me find a ferry from Ibiza to Barcelona on July 11th for 2 adults and 1 child (7 years old)"*
- *"I need a ferry for 4 people from Crete to Santorini next week"*

### Advanced Route Planning

**Island Hopping:**
- *"I want to visit an island within 3 hours of Athens tomorrow and return the next day — what are my options?"*
- *"Plan a ferry route from Athens to Mykonos, then to Santorini, then back to Athens"*
- *"What's the best way to get from Santorini to Crete by ferry?"*

**Multi-Leg Journeys:**
- *"How can I get from Barcelona to Palma de Mallorca and then to Ibiza?"*
- *"Plan a ferry trip from Athens to Paros, then to Naxos, then to Mykonos"*
- *"What's the fastest route from Piraeus to Rhodes?"*

**Regional Exploration:**
- *"Show me all ferry connections from the Greek mainland to the Cyclades islands"*
- *"What ferries connect the Balearic Islands?"*
- *"How can I travel by ferry between the Croatian islands?"*

### Port Discovery

**Port Information:**
- *"What ports are available in the Greek islands?"*
- *"Find ferry ports near Barcelona"*
- *"Show me all ports in the Cyclades"*
- *"What ports serve the Dodecanese islands?"*

**Location-Based Search:**
- *"What's the closest ferry port to Mykonos town?"*
- *"Find ports in the western Greek islands"*
- *"Show me ports that connect to Italy from Greece"*

### Booking and Pricing

**Price Inquiries:**
- *"How much does it cost to take a ferry from Athens to Santorini?"*
- *"What are the prices for a ferry from Barcelona to Ibiza?"*
- *"Show me the cheapest ferry options from Piraeus to Mykonos"*

**Booking Assistance:**
- *"I want to book a ferry from Santorini to Mykonos - can you help me with that?"*
- *"Get me a booking link for the ferry from Athens to Paros"*
- *"How do I book the ferry you just found?"*

## 🎯 Demo Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Last-Minute Island Trip
**User:** *"I'm in Athens and want to visit a Greek island tomorrow. What are my options?"*

**Expected Assistant Response:**
- Search for available ferries from Piraeus to nearby islands
- Present options with departure times and durations
- Provide booking links for selected routes

### Scenario 2: Multi-Island Vacation Planning
**User:** *"I'm planning a 5-day trip to the Greek islands. Start from Athens, visit 2-3 islands, and return to Athens."*

**Expected Assistant Response:**
- Suggest popular island combinations
- Plan ferry routes between islands
- Provide complete itinerary with booking links

### Scenario 3: Family Ferry Trip
**User:** *"I need to get from Barcelona to Ibiza with my family - 2 adults and 2 children. We want to leave on Friday afternoon."*

**Expected Assistant Response:**
- Search for family-friendly ferry options
- Check departure times for Friday afternoon
- Provide pricing and booking information

### Scenario 4: Business Travel
**User:** *"I need to get from Santorini to Mykonos for a business meeting tomorrow morning. What's the earliest ferry?"*

**Expected Assistant Response:**
- Find early morning ferry options
- Check travel times and arrival schedules
- Provide booking links for immediate reservation

## 🎪 Demo Presentation Tips

### Opening Hook
*"Let me show you how our voice assistant can help plan your next island vacation. Watch as I search for ferries across Europe and the Mediterranean in real-time."*

### Key Features to Highlight
1. **Real-time Data**: *"Notice how we're getting live ferry schedules and prices"*
2. **Voice-to-Booking**: *"From voice request to booking link in seconds"*
3. **Complex Planning**: *"Watch as we plan a multi-island journey"*
4. **No Authentication**: *"No API keys needed - it just works"*

### Interactive Demo Flow
1. Start with a simple search: *"Find ferries from Athens to Santorini"*
2. Show complex planning: *"Plan a 3-island trip from Athens"*
3. Demonstrate booking: *"Get me a booking link for that ferry"*
4. Highlight coverage: *"This works across 33 countries and 190+ ferry operators"*

## 🔧 Technical Demo Points

### Integration Benefits
- **No API Keys**: Unlike other services, Ferryhopper requires no authentication
- **Real-time Data**: Live ferry schedules and pricing
- **Global Coverage**: 33 countries, 190+ ferry operators
- **Voice-to-Action**: Complete booking workflow through voice

### Performance Highlights
- **Fast Response**: Quick ferry search results
- **Reliable Service**: Public MCP server with good uptime
- **Rich Data**: Detailed schedules, prices, and booking links
- **Error Handling**: Graceful fallbacks when service is unavailable
